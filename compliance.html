Naet / COMPLIANCE

Deploying in Kenya means meeting Kenyan obligations.

Every institution that deploys Naet is the data controller. This page summarises what the system does to help, and what the institution still has to do. Last reviewed June 2026.

Not legal advice. This is a technical summary of how the system is designed. Confirm your obligations with your institution's counsel and Data Protection Officer.

Who a deploying institution answers to

BodyWhat it requiresHow Naet helps
ODPC
Data Protection Act, 2019		Register as a data controller before going live; appoint a DPO; notify breaches within 72 hours.Role-scoped access, UUID identifiers, consent fields, data export endpoint, admin audit log.
CUE
Universities Act, 2012		Academic and admission records kept accurately and permanently; exportable on audit.Results, enrollment and reporting records; grades frozen at finalisation. A CUE export view is recommended.
KRAFinancial records kept 7 years; receipts that carry the institution PIN, date, amount and description; possible ETR integration.Payment transaction references preserved; payments, accounts and overdrafts never deleted.
HELBVerify and report student enrollment status for loan beneficiaries.The per-semester reporting record supports confirmation. HELB fields and an export endpoint are recommended additions.
KE-CIRT
Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018		Report cyberattacks; protect credentials; unauthorised access is an offence.Hashed passwords, role-scoped admin, and django-axes for lockout and evidence.
NCPWD
Persons with Disabilities Act, 2003		Accessible student portal.Target is WCAG 2.1 AA: semantic HTML, 4.5:1 contrast, keyboard navigation, labelled forms.

Special category data. Religion and ethnicity need explicit consent at registration. If your institution has no functional use for them, remove the fields — every field you don't collect is one you can't be held liable for.

How the system protects it

  • Each role sees only its own jurisdiction: institution, school, department, or the student's own record
  • Passwords hashed; UUIDs in every URL
  • Admin changes logged with user and timestamp
  • HTTPS required in production, with secure cookies and HSTS

What contributors must never do

  • Log or print personal data in plaintext
  • Commit real student data — use anonymised fixtures
  • Add a personal field without documenting its lawful basis
  • Bypass role-based access controls
Minors. For students under 18, the Act requires guardian consent. The design adds an age check at registration and a guardian-consent record for diploma and certificate programmes.

Automated processing. Students have the right to know when a score or decision about them was informed by automated processing. Any analytic flag shown to staff must carry its reasons and be reviewable. See analytics ethics.

If something goes wrong

DATA BREACH

Notify within 72 hours

ODPC · affected individuals if high risk
  • Isolate the affected system
  • Document what was exposed and how many people
  • Notify the ODPC
  • Patch before bringing the system back
CYBERATTACK

Preserve, then report

KE-CIRT · Communications Authority
  • Keep the logs — do not wipe
  • Report to KE-CIRT
  • Engage institution legal counsel
STUDENT COMPLAINT

Direct to the regulator

ODPC complaints desk
  • Students may complain to the ODPC at any time
  • The institution's DPO is first contact

Before going live

LEGAL & REGULATORY

  • ODPC registration completed
  • DPO appointed and contact published
  • CUE registration current
  • KRA PIN registered for fee receipting
  • HELB institution registration confirmed

TECHNICAL

  • HTTPS everywhere — no HTTP in production
  • django-axes installed and configured
  • manage.py check --deploy passes clean
  • Encrypted automated backups
  • Development secret keys rotated
  • Database not publicly accessible

ACCESSIBILITY

  • Semantic HTML throughout
  • Colour contrast 4.5:1 minimum
  • Every form field labelled
  • Keyboard navigation works end to end

DATA

  • Privacy notice on the registration form
  • Consent recorded for special category data
  • Data export endpoint working
  • Retention schedule documented and implemented
  • Staff trained on data handling

Ongoing: review login-attempt logs monthly; renew the ODPC registration, refresh the privacy notice, retrain staff and delete data past retention annually; produce HELB, CUE and KRA exports as requested.
