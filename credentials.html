How the logins are built

Lecturers: first.last@naetuniversity.com or staff number NU/LEC/0001. Password ChangeMe!. The first lecturer in each department is the HOD.

Students: first.last.N@example.com, registration number, or school email. Password ChangeMe123!. The first student in each class is the class rep (REP).

The source of truth is credentials.md, regenerated by the seed script.
