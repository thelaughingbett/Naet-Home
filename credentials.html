How the logins are built

Lecturers

  • Email first.last@naetuniversity.com or staff number NU/LEC/0001
  • Password ChangeMe!
  • The first lecturer in each department is the HOD

Students

  • Email first.last.N@example.com, registration number, or school email
  • Password ChangeMe123!
  • The first student in each class is the class rep (REP)

The source of truth is credentials.md, regenerated by the seed script. This page reads it directly, so it is always current.
