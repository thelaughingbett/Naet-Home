Everything a university runs on, in one data model.

Every module hangs off the same student, the same session and the same curriculum. Below is what exists today, and — honestly marked — what is still on its way.

Academic

The spine: structure, admission, enrollment, grading and graduation. Curriculum — a course, a class and a session — is the hub everything else hangs off.

Academic structure

AVAILABLE

School → Department → Programme → Class, with courses owned by departments and a syllabus linking each programme to its courses.

  • Same course can be core in one programme and elective in another
  • Per-class, per-session lecturer assignment
School · Department · Programme · Tclass · Course · Syllabus · Curriculum

Admissions

AVAILABLE

Applications per programme and session, with documents and transfer-credit evaluation. Acceptance promotes the application into a student record.

  • Supporting documents verified by staff
  • Transfer credits map to a course
Application · ApplicationDocument · TransferCreditEvaluation

Student records

AVAILABLE

Personal, family and emergency details, ID card, per-session reporting, deferment and status-change requests.

  • Online and physical reporting
  • Medical profile with clinic encounters, kept in its own model
Student · Reporting · Deferment · StatusChangeRequest · IDCard

Enrollment

AVAILABLE

An explicit record linking a student to a curriculum entry. Core and common units are enrolled automatically when a student reports; electives need approval.

  • HOD or course lecturer approves electives
  • Signal-driven, so student creation stays decoupled
Enrollment

Grading & results

AVAILABLE

One grading rulebook for every screen: weighted components, grade bands, pass marks, and grades frozen at finalisation.

  • No partial scores — a unit is "in progress" until complete
  • Exam and project marks need HOD approval before release
  • GPA, CGPA, classification and progression computed centrally
Result · Assessment plan · Grading scale

Degree audit & graduation

AVAILABLE

Publishing a result recalculates credits and GPA. Eligible students are nominated, verified by the HOD, approved by the Registrar, then issued a diploma and seated at convocation.

DegreeAudit · Graduation · Diploma · Convocation

Academic requisitions

AVAILABLE

Resit, supplementary, special exam, missing marks and re-evaluation requests share one two-stage review: HOD recommendation, then Registrar decision.

  • Duplicate open requests blocked
  • Rejections require a written reason
  • Fee gate before completion where a fee applies
AcademicRequisition · RequisitionDocument

Course & lecturer evaluations

AVAILABLE

Students rate each curriculum entry and its lecturers inside a dedicated evaluation window before exams.

CourseEvaluation · LecturerEvaluation

Finance

What a student owes comes from their class and the session, never from a hand-typed figure. The balance is derived from payments, charges, awards and refunds. The module is under consideration to move into a dedicated financial ERP, with the event layer keeping them in sync.

Fee structures & accounts

AVAILABLE

Itemised fee structures per class and session (tuition, registration, hostel and more) and a per-student account with real-time balance.

FeeStructure · StudentFeeAccount

Payments

AVAILABLE

M-Pesa, bank transfer and cash recorded against the account, confirmed by the provider, with a transaction reference preserved for receipting. The payment screen adapts to each connector: a phone prompt (STK push), a redirect to the provider's secure page, or a manual entry at the finance office.

  • Pluggable provider contract — a new provider needs no screen changes
  • Every provider callback is verified before it is believed
  • Balances are always derived, never typed in
  • If a provider fails, nothing is posted to the ledger
Payment

Charges, scholarships & refunds

AVAILABLE

Fines and late fees, disbursed scholarships, and a refund flow for overpayments from approval to completion. Overdrafts carry forward at session rollover.

General ledger postings

AVAILABLE

Confirmed payments and completed refunds post journal entries (Dr Cash · Cr Receivable on payment).

Scheduling & exams

Timetabling

AVAILABLE

Curriculum + venue + time slot, with venue and lecturer clash detection and shared teaching slots across programmes. A scheduler proposes the timetable, the proposal is checked, and only then is it saved.

  • Common units are placed where every lecturer and class involved is free
  • If a course can't be placed, the run names the course and class and saves nothing partial
  • Re-running replaces the session's schedule, so hand adjustments come after generating
  • The default scheduler is greedy and swappable for an optimisation solver by configuration
  • Each class session signed off by the class rep, with reschedules recorded
Building · Venue · Timetable · DailyClassExecution

Examinations

AVAILABLE

Exam coordinators prepare sittings per curriculum and nominate invigilators; the Director of Examinations approves a consolidated timetable after a clash check across every sitting.

  • Workload limits enforced on invigilators
  • No student, class, venue or invigilator is double-booked at the same date and slot
  • The unit's own lecturers are preferred as invigilators
  • The exam scheduler is all-or-nothing: if one exam can't be placed, none are saved
  • QR-verified exam cards, gated on fee status
ExamSession · ExamVenue · ExamInvigilatorAssignment · ExamClash · ExamCard

Case handling

A case sits at one level at a time, and only whoever holds that level can act on it. Time limits escalate it automatically, vacant seats are skipped, and every step is on a timeline.

Complaints & grievances

AVAILABLE

Four category ladders — Results, Academic, Facilities/Administrative and Harassment — each with its own time limits. Faculty-level handling is live for Results and Academic complaints.

  • Results disputes place the marks on hold and record them as filed
  • Harassment goes straight to the Dean of Students
  • Anonymous complaints stay anonymous to faculty
Complaint · ComplaintEscalationHistory · ComplaintNote

Appeals

AVAILABLE

A closed decision can be appealed within 14 days, up to twice, with written grounds. The case reopens one level up with the original decision preserved.

ComplaintAppeal

Staff inbox

AVAILABLE

My cases and History tabs, ordered by deadline, with an awaiting / overdue / in-progress / resolved summary. Acknowledge, note, resolve, reject or escalate.

Higher-level routing

PLANNED

Dean, student-council seats, Dean of Students, Registrar and Senate levels are added as their screens are built. Requisition review will move onto the same engine so result entrants and liaisons see remarking requests first.

Campus life

Hostels

AVAILABLE

Allocation by room capacity and gender, a waitlist when full, warden review of end-of-session evaluations, and a separate directory of off-campus listings.

Hostel · Room · HostelAllocation · HostelEvaluation · HostelListing

Clubs & societies

AVAILABLE

Recruitment drives, membership approval, leadership terms, budgets and transactions, activity reports and patron oversight. Events go to the Dean of Students for sign-off.

Club · ClubMembership · ClubEvent · ClubBudget

Student council

AVAILABLE

Terms, elections with vetted candidates and one vote per student per seat, proposals with collected signatures, meetings with attendance, and portfolio-routed grievances.

CouncilTerm · Election · Vote · CouncilProposal · Grievance

Compliance & communications

Accreditation & regulatory reports

AVAILABLE

Programme and institution-wide accreditations with evidence documents, and regulatory reports tied to a session.

Accreditation · RegulatoryReport

Registration windows & calendar

AVAILABLE

Fourteen windows — reporting, course registration, fees, hostel, bursary, elections, exams, backlog — generated from fixed offsets so every new session gets a consistent calendar. A derived compliance calendar projects every deadline.

RegistrationWindow · ComplianceCalendarEvent

Announcements

AVAILABLE

Staff publish notices, optionally scoped to a single curriculum entry, with attachments.

News & events

AVAILABLE

Card-only feeds pulled from WordPress, RSS or any CMS every 30 minutes, with live and manual fallbacks. Cancelled events are hidden, never erased.

Integration

Anything slow or external runs in the background. See the architecture for how the pattern works.

ERP sync

AVAILABLE

Events such as payment.confirmed or enrollment.approved queue after commit. Each connector retries independently with exponential backoff; every attempt is logged with status, message, external reference and raw response.

ERPSyncLog

Notifications

AVAILABLE

Email and SMS through pluggable backends, kept inside the opt-in expectations of the Kenya Information and Communications Act.

Results import

AVAILABLE

Bring CAT and exam scores in from a lecturer's Excel sheet or a push from a learning system such as Moodle or Canvas. A connector only reads and translates; the portal alone validates and writes.

  • Only students with an approved enrollment for the unit receive a score
  • Unmatched or malformed rows warn and skip; an unreadable file fails and imports nothing
  • Re-importing the same file updates results, never duplicates them
  • Imported scores follow the normal approval and release rules

PDF engine

AVAILABLE

Transcripts, invoices and other documents are rendered through a swappable engine. Headless Chromium is the reference implementation; WeasyPrint, ReportLab or your own engine can sit behind the same contract.

  • Each document type is mapped to an engine in settings, with no view changes
  • An engine failure comes back as a clear result, never a crash
